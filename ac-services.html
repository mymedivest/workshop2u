How often should I service my vehicle's A/C system?

Your car's air conditioning is a sealed system, so fluid is only lost through a leak. Having it evaluated and recharged about every three years is a good maintenance guideline, even if it seems to work well.

Let us help fix your car

The A/C constantly converts refrigerant from liquid to cold mist to hot gas and back again. The most common causes of failure are leaks or compressor issues. If it blows cool but not cold, the cause could be a clogged filter or the cooling fan.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

How often should I service my car's A/C?
Every 3 years is recommended, or sooner if the system is blowing warm air.
What are common A/C problems?
Leaks, compressor issues, clogged filters or faulty cooling fans.
What does an A/C performance check include?
Visual inspection, refrigerant charge check, electronic leak test and system control test.
Why choose certified technicians?
They have the expertise to diagnose and fix A/C issues correctly, safely and efficiently.
