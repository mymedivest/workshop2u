Frequently asked questions

What types of vehicles do you service?
Sedans, hatchbacks, SUVs, MPVs, vans and pickup trucks.
What is included in the car wash service?
Each package includes a complete car wash, in-car vacuum and tyre polish.
How much does the car wash cost?
  • Sedan / Hatchback: RM 10
  • SUV / MPV: RM 15
  • Van / Pickup Truck: RM 15
Do I need to make an appointment?
Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking in advance to ensure availability and avoid waiting.
How long does a car wash take?
About 20–30 minutes, depending on vehicle size and condition.
Where is your car wash service located?
At our workshop. Visit us directly or contact us for location and hours.
