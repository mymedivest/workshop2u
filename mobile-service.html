We come to you

With Workshop2U Mobile Service Onsite, you no longer need to visit the workshop for minor servicing. Our trained technicians come to your home, office, project site or parking area to carry out selected maintenance professionally and efficiently.

Why choose Mobile Service Onsite?

  • Service at your preferred location
  • Save time, with no workshop visit
  • Suitable for personal vehicles and company fleets
  • Certified and experienced technicians
  • Professional tools and safe procedures
  • Transparent pricing

Services available

Minor services only

  • Engine oil and oil filter replacement
  • Basic vehicle inspection
  • Fluid top-up and checks
  • Battery inspection and replacement
  • Brake inspection (visual)
  • Minor troubleshooting and diagnostics

Major repairs, heavy mechanical work and complex diagnostics are not included. For major issues, our team will advise servicing at our workshop.

Service locations

  • Residential homes
  • Office buildings
  • Company parking areas
  • Project sites
  • Fleet and commercial vehicle locations

Coverage is subject to location and accessibility.

Key benefits

Regular maintenance keeps everything working properly and stops minor issues becoming costly problems. Mobile Service Onsite is for preventive care and minor servicing only.

  • Customised minor services for your vehicle
  • Eco-friendly products where applicable
  • Reliable, timely service at your location
  • All tools and equipment provided
  • No workshop waiting
  • Better efficiency through basic maintenance

Pricing and appointment

Pricing depends on the type of minor service, vehicle model and service location. The final price is confirmed before service begins.

Frequently asked questions

What services are included in Mobile Service Onsite?
Minor maintenance at your location: engine oil changes, fluid checks and top-ups, battery inspection and replacement, brake inspection and basic diagnostics. Major repairs and complex mechanical work are not available onsite.
How often should I service my vehicle?
Minor services such as oil changes are typically recommended every 5,000–10,000 km. Our technician can advise the best schedule.
Can the mobile service van replace my car battery?
Yes. The van carries batteries and the tools to inspect and replace your battery at your location.
What makes Mobile Service Onsite convenient?
The van comes to your home, office or project site, so you don't need to travel. The service is fast and done by trained, experienced technicians.
Are the technicians reliable?
Yes. All are trained, certified and experienced, using proper tools and professional procedures.
