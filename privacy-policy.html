Introduction

Effective date: 20 November 2024.

Workshop2u and its group companies (“we”, “us”) respect the privacy of everyone who uses our websites and mobile apps and protect your Personal Data in line with Malaysian law, including the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

“Personal Data” means any information that relates directly or indirectly to an identifiable individual, including sensitive personal data and expressions of opinion. By giving us your Personal Data, you consent to its collection, use, handling, retention and disclosure as described here.

Our collection of your Personal Data

We collect data directly from you when you:

  • Register for an account on our website or app
  • Contact us for information
  • Communicate with us by any means
  • Interact with our website or app
  • Join promotions or feedback surveys

We may also collect data indirectly from third parties such as referees, public records or background check agencies. If you do not provide the data we request, we may be unable to provide or continue providing our services.

The extent and type of Personal Data we collect

This depends on the services you use and may include:

  • Personal information: name, NRIC or passport, address, date of birth, photograph, vehicle registration number, location, driver's licence, insurance details
  • Contact information: addresses, telephone and email
  • Transaction and financial details: services used, card and bank details
  • Device information: hardware, software, network, IMEI, UUID, model
  • E-wallet information: balance, top-up and transaction history
  • Log information: geolocation, browser, IP address, diagnostics
  • Feedback and opinions: comments, reviews and ratings
  • Sensitive data: health, political opinions or criminal records, only where applicable and with your consent

If you are a company, you must obtain your employees' consent before giving us their Personal Data.

Use of Personal Data collected

We may use your data to:

  • Identify and manage users and accounts, and provide our services (including partner services you choose)
  • Process transactions and handle administration and record-keeping
  • Research, analyse and improve our website and app, and publish your reviews for marketing
  • Seek your opinions and send promotions, updates and newsletters
  • Run diagnostics, software updates, maintenance and technical support
  • Respond to inquiries, concerns and complaints
  • Verify identity and run due diligence, background and risk checks
  • Detect the location of your vehicle or service equipment for safety and security
  • Administer contests, campaigns and rewards
  • Comply with legal obligations and requests from authorities
  • Support mergers, acquisitions, financing or similar transactions, and any other purpose related to your dealings with us or permitted by law

Processing of Personal Data

Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time by emailing us (see Contact Us below). Withdrawal does not affect processing already done. We may also process data on other lawful grounds under the PDPA, such as fraud detection or legal obligations, without seeking your consent.

Sharing and disclosure

We may disclose your data, within or outside Malaysia, to parties who reasonably need it, including:

  • Workshop2u group companies
  • Contractors, sub-contractors and business or strategic partners
  • Banks, card issuers, payment gateways and payment service providers
  • Search engine and analytics providers
  • Advertisers and advertising networks
  • Entities assisting fraud investigations
  • Parties to corporate exercises such as mergers, acquisitions or funding, and their advisers
  • Parties in legal proceedings, and governmental, regulatory or law enforcement bodies
  • Any other third party we tell you about at collection, with your consent where required

Storage of your Personal Data

We may process, store or transfer your data outside Malaysia, taking reasonable steps to keep it protected. Only employees who need access to do their jobs can see it, and they must keep it confidential. We use technical and organisational security measures that follow industry standards, but cannot guarantee that loss, misuse or unauthorised alteration will never occur.

Access to, correction or deletion of your Personal Data

You can view and update your data by logging into your account. If you cannot, send a request through the form provided. We may charge a reasonable fee under the Personal Data Protection (Fees) Regulations 2013 and may refuse a request where the law allows, in which case we will explain in writing.

To delete your account or withdraw consent, go to App > Account > View and Edit Profile > Delete Account. Deleting an account on one of our websites or apps deletes your accounts across all of them.

Cookies

Cookies are small data files sent to your device when you visit a website. We use them to recognise your activity and personalise your experience. You can set your device to warn you before accepting cookies, reject them or turn them off. If you keep using our website or app without changing these settings, you accept cookies.

External and third-party websites

Our website and app may link to third-party sites or social media channels. We do not operate, control or endorse them. You are responsible for your use of those sites and should read their privacy notices and terms.

Changes to our Privacy Notice

We may update this notice at any time. Updates are posted on our website or app and replace the previous version. By continuing to use our services after an update, you agree to the revised terms.

Discrepancies

This notice is available in English and Bahasa Malaysia. If the two differ, the English version prevails.

Contact us

For questions about this notice or your Personal Data (except account deletion requests), email customercare@workshop2u.com.my.
