Frequently asked questions

What's included in the normal service package?
Essential maintenance such as engine oil replacement, an oil filter change and a general inspection to make sure everything is running smoothly.
How often should I get a normal service?
Every 5,000 to 10,000 km, depending on driving conditions.
What's the difference between semi-synthetic and fully synthetic oil?
Semi-synthetic oil is a mix of mineral and synthetic oil, with good performance at a lower cost. Fully synthetic oil is entirely synthetic, giving better engine protection and efficiency.
What are the benefits of preventative maintenance?
It catches potential issues early, helping you avoid costly repairs, keep the car efficient and prolong its life.
What should I do before a normal service?
Park in a safe location and make sure the car has enough fuel to be driven to the service centre. Our team takes care of the rest.
Why choose Workshop2U's certified technicians?
Our team is certified and trained to handle a variety of vehicles with care, using the latest technology for quality service every time.
