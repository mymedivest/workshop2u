Expert repairs help extend the life of your vehicle by addressing issues accurately before they turn into major problems. With proper tools, skilled technicians and quality parts, repairs restore performance and prevent future damage, keeping your car reliable, safe and valuable for years.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between minor and major service?
A minor service covers basic maintenance such as oil changes. A major service is a more thorough check-up of all critical systems and components.
How often should I get a major service?
Typically every 30,000 to 50,000 km, or annually, depending on your vehicle and driving conditions.
Does major service include parts replacement?
Yes, essential parts such as spark plugs, filters and fluids are replaced as needed.
Why is major service important?
Regular major maintenance prevents expensive repairs by addressing issues early, keeping your car efficient and safe.
What can I expect during a major service?
A comprehensive inspection and servicing covering fluid levels, brake systems, engine components and more.
